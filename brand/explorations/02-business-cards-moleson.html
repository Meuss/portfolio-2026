Print details I’ll handle in the final files

  • Size: 85 × 55 mm trim, 3 mm bleed on every side (91 × 61 mm document), text kept 4 mm inside the edge.
  • Paper: 350–400 g/m² uncoated or matte. Red full-bleed designs (V2, V3, V5) look much richer on uncoated stock.
  • Colour: the closest Pantone to the red is 485 C. I’ll give you a CMYK PDF, and a Pantone version if your printer offers it.
  • Text as outlines: no fonts needed at the print shop.
