Round 2 · black, white, orange · not the mountain

Twelve more ideas, now in orange

The mountain logo and its business cards are saved, untouched. This sheet tries different ideas with your colours. Each idea is shown big on white, then on black, on orange, and as an icon at app size and at 16 pixels, the size of a browser tab.

Black #121212 White #ffffff Orange #ff6a13 · ≈ Pantone 1585 C

Point final

O1

Lowercase name with an orange full stop. Simple, confident, and it suits a short sentence like “Done.”

Mi||er

O2 My pick

The two l’s in Miller become orange bars. They read as letters, as code pipes, or as a pause symbol. The bars alone make a strong icon.

Sélection

O3

Your name selected like an element in a design tool, handles and all. Speaks to people who build websites. Busy at small sizes.

Curseur

O4

Your initials typed in a code font with an orange cursor block. Clearly a developer, still friendly.

T/M

O5

Tall condensed initials split by an orange slash, like a URL path. Bold and poster-like.

Pastille

O6

An orange disc with your initials, like a sticker. Warm and approachable. Ready-made round avatar.

Pixels

O7

TM drawn on a pixel grid with an orange square as the full stop. A nod to screens and early computers. Pixel-perfect as a favicon.

Grille

O8

Your name as a 3 × 4 block of letters, initials in orange. Swiss-poster feel, perfectly square. Too detailed for a 16 px icon.

Étiquette

O9

Your name on a strip of orange tape, slightly crooked. Handmade and fun. Less serious, which can be a good thing.

Ligature

O10

The T’s orange crossbar runs straight into the M, so the initials read as one built piece. Solid and engineered.

Chargement

O11

Initials inside a three-quarter ring, like a loading spinner. A wink at web work. The ring could animate on your site.

Moléson, en orange

O12

For comparison: your current mountain logo in the new colours. If this one wins, the idea was right and only the red was wrong.

How to react

  • Name two or three numbers you like, even for one detail (“the bars from O2 with the font of O1”).
  • Tell me what you dislike. It narrows things down as much as a favourite does.
  • One thing to know about orange: orange text on white is hard to read in small sizes, so in every idea orange is a shape or a big accent, never body text.
