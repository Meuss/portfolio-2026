What the print files will include

  • Size: 85 × 55 mm, plus 3 mm extra on every side that the printer trims off (91 × 61 mm files). Text stays 4 mm inside the cut.
  • Files: one PDF per side, CMYK, text converted to shapes so no fonts are needed.
  • Orange: close to Pantone 1585 C. Bright orange shifts a lot in CMYK, so ask for a printed proof, or use Pantone printing if the shop offers it.
  • Paper: 350–400 g/m² matte. Black cards (K2, K4) look best with soft-touch lamination.
