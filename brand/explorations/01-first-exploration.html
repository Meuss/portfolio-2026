Recommendation

My pick: B1, the Moléson M

Thomas MillerDéveloppeur web · Bulle
  • It has a symbol. As a freelancer you need a favicon, a LinkedIn avatar and a GitHub picture. A pure wordmark can’t do that job.
  • It’s about where you are. Local clients hire local people. The Moléson is on every view from Bulle, and the peaks only read as mountains once you know, so it stays subtle.
  • It doesn’t say “coder” too loudly. Your clients buy websites, not code. Brackets and terminals speak to other developers.
  • It’s cheap to live with. One red, one font, one shape. It works on white, on black, on red, and in one colour on a rubber stamp.

Still unsure?

Three questions that settle it

  1. Who pays you? Local businesses in Gruyère point to B1, A1 or C2. Agencies and startups that hire you as a contractor point to A4, B3 or C4.
  2. Do you want to sound like a person or a studio? A person: C1, A1. A studio you might grow later: A2, B2.
  3. Will you ever need a tiny version? If yes (and you will), pick from family B or C3, or add a monogram to the wordmark you like.

Why there is no Swiss cross anywhere: the cross and the Swiss flag are protected by federal law, and using them in a trademark is heavily restricted. The red alone carries the reference safely.
